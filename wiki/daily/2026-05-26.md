## Gotchas learned

- React 19 `react-hooks/refs` blocks reading `ref.current` during render. Clock state had to migrate from `useRef` to `useState` + `setState` inside effect with local `react-hooks/set-state-in-effect` disable per the established pattern.
- `useReducedMotion` hook needed `useSyncExternalStore` (not `useEffect + setState`) to satisfy React 19's `react-hooks/set-state-in-effect` rule.
- `next dev` rewrites quotes in `next-env.d.ts` on every boot; revert before staging.
